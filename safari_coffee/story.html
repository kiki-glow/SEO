Our Story

One roaster.
No middleman.

2025, we drove to the slopes of Mt. Kenya looking for beans. Found teh Kamau family. They grow SL28 at 1900m, hand-pick only red cherries.

We pay 2.5x fair trade, roast in a 5kg Probat in Makutano, and rest beans 10 days. Most cafes serve after 2 days — that's why its bitter. Time is an ingredient.
